Social Generator

A tool that produces short vertical videos and publishes them to the social media accounts its operator connects.

What it does

  • Writes a script, generates narration and images, and renders a 9:16 video.
  • Schedules and publishes the video to the connected YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  • On TikTok, sends the video as a draft to the account's inbox, or posts it directly after the account owner reviews it and confirms on the “Post to TikTok” screen.

Accounts

Accounts are connected through each platform's own authorization screen (for TikTok, “Continue with TikTok” via Login Kit) and can be disconnected at any time.
