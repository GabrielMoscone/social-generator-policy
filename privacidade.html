Privacy Policy — Social Generator

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. About this app

Social Generator is a tool that produces short vertical videos (narration, images and rendering) and publishes them to social media accounts that the person operating it has connected: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. It has no public sign-up. The only people whose data it handles are the account owners who explicitly connect their accounts through each platform's own authorization screen.

2. Data we access

PlatformPermissionWhat it is used for
TikTokuser.info.basic Read the account's open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account will receive a post.
TikTokvideo.publish Publish a video only after the account owner reviews it and presses “Post”, with the privacy level, interaction settings and content disclosure they chose. Also used to read the creator's posting settings and the status of a post.
TikTokvideo.upload Send a video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft, for the owner to finish in the TikTok app.
YouTubeyoutube.upload, youtube.readonly Upload videos to the connected channel and read view, like and comment counts of those videos.
Facebook / InstagramPage and Instagram content publishing Publish Reels to the connected Page and Instagram account.

The app does not read followers, messages, the content of comments, other users' data, or any content that the connected account did not publish through this app. It does not use any data for advertising, profiling, or training of machine-learning models, and it does not sell or share data.

3. Data we store

  • Access and refresh tokens for each connected account, and their expiry time, so that scheduled posts keep working.
  • Account identifiers: the platform's account ID (for TikTok, the open_id) and the display name.
  • Post records: which video was sent to which account, when, the choices made on the posting screen, the publish ID returned by the platform, and the result or error.
  • The videos themselves, which are produced by the app.

The creator's avatar and posting settings returned by TikTok are fetched each time the posting screen opens and are not stored.

4. Where data is kept

Videos are produced and kept on the operator's computer. Tokens, account identifiers and post records are kept in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon. A rendered video may be stored temporarily in Cloudflare R2 so that scheduled posts can be sent while the computer is off, and those scheduled jobs run on GitHub Actions. These providers only host the data; they do not receive it for their own purposes.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept while the account remains connected. Disconnecting an account in the app's settings deletes its tokens and its post records.
  • A video copy in Cloudflare R2 is deleted once it has been published to all destinations.
  • Deleting a channel in the app deletes its accounts, tokens and post records.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the platform itself: in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services; for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for Meta under Settings → Business integrations. Revoking immediately stops all access.

To request deletion of any data related to your account, write to the contact below and it will be removed within 30 days.

6. Security

Tokens are never shown in the app's interface after being saved, and are transmitted only over HTTPS. When the app's interface is reachable from the internet, it is protected by a password.

7. Platform terms

Use of each platform through this app is also subject to that platform's terms: the TikTok Terms of Service, the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy, and the Meta Privacy Policy. This app uses YouTube API Services.
